Features

Everything you need to stay on track

💊

Medication Tracking

Dosage, frequency, food requirements, prescriber info. Never miss a dose with timed reminders.

📋

Medical Instructions

Post-surgery care, wound care, doctor's orders — broken into steps with individual reminders.

📅

Appointments

Multi-stage reminders (1 day, 2 hours before) with prep checklists so you're always ready.

📊

Health Metrics

Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight — with target ranges, out-of-range alerts, and trend tracking.

🔄

Refill Alerts

Auto-calculates when your supply runs out and alerts you days before so you never run dry.

🔒

Privacy First

All data stored locally in a single JSON file. Nothing leaves your machine. You own your health data.

How It Works

Natural language in, smart reminders out

1

Tell Your Agent

"I take NMN 500mg every morning" or "Doctor said no heavy lifting for 6 weeks"

2

Agent Confirms

Parses your input into structured data and confirms the details with you

3

Reminders Activate

Cron jobs auto-schedule to your preferred channel — Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp

4

Track & Review

Log adherence, check trends, get refill alerts. Ask "health status" anytime.
