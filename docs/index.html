✦ Features

Everything your agent needs
to manage your health

Six powerful capabilities in one lightweight skill. No cloud, no accounts — just your agent and your data.

💊

Medication Tracking

Track prescriptions and supplements with dosage, frequency, timing, and special instructions. Never miss a dose.

📋

Medical Instructions

Store doctor's orders, dietary restrictions, and care protocols. Your agent recalls them when relevant.

📅

Appointments

Schedule and track doctor visits, lab work, and follow-ups with smart reminders ahead of time.

📊

Health Metrics

Log blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, and custom vitals. Spot trends over time.

🔔

Refill Alerts

Automatic reminders when medications are running low. Configure supply tracking per medication.

🔒

Privacy-First

All data stored locally as JSON files. No cloud sync, no third parties. Your health data stays yours.
