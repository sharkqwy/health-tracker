✦ Features

Everything your agent needs
to manage your health

Six capabilities in one lightweight skill. No cloud, no accounts, no subscriptions — just your agent and your data stored locally.

💊

Medication Tracking

Track prescriptions and supplements with dosage, frequency, timing, and special instructions. Never miss a dose again.

📋

Medical Instructions

Store doctor's orders, dietary restrictions, and care protocols. Your agent recalls them when relevant.

📅

Appointment Management

Schedule and track doctor visits, lab work, and follow-ups with smart reminders ahead of time.

📊

Health Metrics & Vitals

Log blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, and custom vitals. Spot trends and share reports with your doctor.

🔔

Refill Alerts

Automatic reminders when medications are running low. Configure supply tracking per medication so you never run out.

🔒

Privacy-First Design

All data stored locally as JSON files. No cloud sync, no third-party access. Your health data never leaves your machine.

→ How It Works

Set up in four simple steps

Just tell your agent what you're taking. It handles the rest — no forms, no data entry.

  1. 💬

    Tell Your Agent

    "I take metformin 500mg twice daily with meals"

  2. ✅

    Agent Confirms & Stores

    Parses your input and saves structured data locally as JSON

  3. ⏰

    Reminders Activate

    Cron-based reminders fire at the right times automatically

  4. 📈

    Track & Review

    Ask your agent for summaries, trends, and medication schedules

🤖 Agent-Native Architecture

Built for AI agents, not humans with GUIs

Not another health app. This is infrastructure for AI agents that care for humans — lightweight, inspectable, and reliable.

No databases, no APIs, no vendor lock-in. Plain JSON files your agent reads and writes directly. Inspect, edit, or back up anytime.
